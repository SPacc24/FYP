# 30-Second Product Positioning

## Say this (memorise)

> AutoPenTest is a **lab pentest orchestrator** for authorised cyber ranges.  
> It turns scan evidence into ATT&CK-tagged playbooks, auto-runs only **low-risk** checks, **suppresses** unsupported celebrity exploits, and requires a human plus a **proof artefact** before impact or pivot.  
> AI may explain — it never invents exploit modules.  
> First users: **training / cyber-range operators**, not enterprises replacing BAS vendors.

## Do not say

- “AI penetration tester”
- “Fully autonomous red team”
- “We eliminate hardcoding”
- “We discover zero-days”
- “Better than Pentera/Tenable out of the box”

## Primary market (one)

Cyber-range operators · security course labs · internal purple-team practice ranges

## Secondary (later, unclaimed today)

MSSP multi-tenant · cloud attack paths · continuous BAS

## Competitor honesty frame

| Class | Relation |
|-------|----------|
| Vuln scanners | We consume their style of evidence; we don't replace them |
| MSF / CALDERA | Execution providers under our gate |
| Commercial BAS | Mature ops products; we are a transparent teaching/research MVP |
| Pure LLM agents | Opposite philosophy — catalog custody beats freeform tooling |
